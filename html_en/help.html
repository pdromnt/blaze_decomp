▲▼　Controls　▲▼
[Z] tapNormal shot (can cancel out enemy bullets)
[Z] holdSpecial weapon (Vulcan if no item has been collected)
[X] holdDash (Back Fire also becomes a weapon)
[X] double-clickRing (MODE 03: weapon switch)
[Z][X] togetherMega Crash (up to 3 uses per stage)
[Z][X] releasedShield recovery (except in MODE 02)
[Arrow keys]Movement
▲▼　How to Play　▲▼
MODE 00Defeated enemies drop items. When you pick one up, an energy gauge appears beneath your craft, and you can fire special attacks until it runs out.
MODE 01Choose a special weapon at the start. Energy regenerates gradually; if you exhaust it, the weapon is unusable until it finishes recharging.
MODE 02The special weapon has no limit. Fire away as much as you like — but your shield will not regenerate. (For advanced players.)
MODE 03Special weapons can be swapped. Double-click [X] to select one.
OPTIONConfigure the game
GAME LEVEL: Set the difficulty
SCREEN MODE: Set the screen mode. Mode 3 is the best choice, but if it doesn't work, try the other modes. Full Screen 5 is heavier, but tends to work on most machines.
EXITQuit the game
ScoreDefeating enemies adds to your score. The faster you take them out, the more points you earn. Wiping out every enemy in a wave also grants a bonus.
A ranking page is published on the official website.
