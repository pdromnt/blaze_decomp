▲▼　STAGE 1　▲▼

BLAZE touches down on Planet Ged, the very world where this nightmare began.
It must head for the facility that the Seventh Base "Ende" had been excavating
and swiftly uncover the cause of the incident.
However, the revived system keeps producing the unmanned fighters used in the previous war, blocking BLAZE's path...


ERISU — Anti-Extraterrestrial Humanoid Weapon Prototype
One of the ultimate weapons developed to fight off outer-space lifeforms, and the earliest one built.
Equipped with a diffusion energy-wave cannon and a large-caliber energy cannon.
During development, some argued that a humanoid design was largely pointless.
▲▼　STAGE 2　▲▼

All that remained in the facility was the humanoid weapon ERISU.
As ERISU falls silent, the facility begins to self-destruct, and BLAZE escapes.
But its radar has already detected a new force closing in.

FERIA — Multi-Purpose Atmospheric Fighter
Despite an outward appearance reminiscent of older weapons, FERIA is a cutting-edge fighter packed with the latest equipment.
Its primary role is patrol, but it is also tasked with exterminating any target it discovers.
Features a tough body and an array of weaponry.
▲▼　STAGE 3　▲▼

Just before being destroyed, FERIA transmitted an SOS signal.
Its destination was outer space... the awakened anti-extraterrestrial weapon had already left Ged.
BLAZE gives chase, but the enemy's attacks only grow fiercer.

SERISU — Deep-Space Fleet-Annihilation Battleship
A massive warship, a weapon built to wipe out outer-space lifeforms.
Loaded with large-scale armaments such as homing lasers and giant cannons.
But its true purpose is to serve as RAIYA's escort.
▲▼　STAGE 4　▲▼

With the destruction of SERISU, space itself begins to warp.
The ultimate weapon RAIYA has been hiding within a parallel dimension.
BLAZE charges into its final battle.

RAIYA — Central Core of the Anti-Extraterrestrial Ultimate Weapon
The ultimate weapon, fitted with a device that manipulates space-time.
Its power was said to reach humanity itself, and multiple seals had been placed upon it, but...
Naturally, the strongest of all.
