▲▼　Story　▲▼
A crisis has come to the long-peaceful Cosmic Federation.
The awakening of an ultimate weapon that had been buried during the war with invaders from outer space!!
Existing military forces are no match for its lost super-scientific power.
Deeming the Federation its enemy, the ultimate weapon is distorting space-time, and its influence has even begun to reach the soul...
At this rate, humanity will be annihilated!!

The Federation Council sends out a craft that embodies the pinnacle of super-science, staking the very survival of the human race.

The craft's name is BLAZE.


A "raging flame" — the last beacon-fire left to humanity...
